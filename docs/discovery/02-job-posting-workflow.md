# 02 — Real workflow from job postings

Desk-research note 2 of 6 (`W1-DR-01`). Sources: US bank job postings found via job-search indexing,
Oct 2026. Customer SIMULATED.

## Sources

- Customers Bank, **Compliance Complaint Management Specialist** (careers.banktechventures.com; posting since
  closed) — the most detailed posting; duties read verbatim below.
- SoFi, **Complaints Analyst, Deposits Compliance** — "second-line independent oversight and critical
  assessment/guidance on customer complaints related to consumer deposit products."
- Citizens Bank, **Collections & Recovery Complaints Analyst** — "identifying, investigating, and resolving
  issues driving escalated customer complaints, legal cases, operational losses, and regulatory risk."
- Market size (Oct 2026 indexing): Indeed ~7,600 "compliance complaint management analyst" roles; LinkedIn
  8,000+ "complaints management" banking roles in the US.
- CFPB complaint process page (consumerfinance.gov/complaint/process): companies "generally respond in 15
  days"; if in progress, a final response within 60 days; consumer feedback window 60 days.

## The daily workflow, synthesized across postings

1. **Intake and routing** — complaints arrive via the regulator channel (CFPB portal) plus executive and other
   escalated paths; each gets investigated.
2. **Investigation with the business** — coordinate with business units, gather documentation, assess customer
   impact.
3. **Root cause + fix** — "identify root cause issues" and devise fixes "for individual customers and systemic
   problems."
4. **Response memo** — craft summary/response memos "to Management, the Bank's regulator" and escalated
   complainants.
5. **Sampling and risk indicators** — "perform sampling of complaints to identify risk indicators."
6. **Reporting and tracking** — monthly reporting, complaint summaries, issue tracking, audit support.

Customers Bank verbatim duties (abbreviated): evaluate customer complaints for the bank *and its vendors*;
root cause both individual and systemic; sampling for risk indicators; coordinate fixes across business units;
monthly reporting and issue tracking; support audits; context framed as "prevention and detection of regulatory
non-compliance and/or consumer harm." Requirements: 3–5 years compliance/risk/ops/customer advocacy; tools:
Word, Excel, Outlook. No SLAs named in the posting; no team size.

## What it means for Complaint Intelligence

- **The persona is real and correctly shaped:** the postings describe exactly the brief's persona — a team
  working regulator-routed complaints, escalating the risky ones, reporting upward on volumes and emerging
  issues. First line (ops) with a compliance oversight layer above (SoFi's "second-line" posting).
- **Scarce senior attention is implied, not stated:** no posting admits "we can't review everything," but
  "sampling of complaints to identify risk indicators" is the manual version of a risk score — H1 is the
  automation of a step banks already do by hand.
- **The 15-day/60-day portal clocks plus Reg E (10 business days / 45) and Reg Z (30 days / 2 cycles) stacks
  deadlines** — this is the clock the persona actually manages; timeliness of the *CFPB response* (99.55% in
  note 01) is the easy half.
- **A draft-able artifact exists:** "summary and response memos to Management and the regulator" is a concrete
  agent output (propose, human edits and sends) that fits the Foundry-action model: propose-action tool,
  write-back only via logged actions.
- **Tooling is Excel-grade** — a Workshop triage queue with Ontology-backed history is a credible upgrade, not
  a solution in search of a problem.

## Limits

Postings are employer marketing; closed/inactive at fetch time; snippets via search indexing rather than the
full ATS records. Volumes (7,600 roles) are index counts, not curated counts.
